Biography Of Shah Fahad

Early Life

Shah Fahad was born in a small village of Pakistan. His father was a shopkeeper and his mother was a housewife. Growing in this village, he taught the importance of hardwork. So he dedicated himself to studies and he proved to be a brilliant student.

Career

For the higher studies, he moved to Saudi Arabia. After graduating from here, he first worked as a developer and then project manager and after 3 years became the CEO of that leading company.

Achievements

He made a lot of achievements in his student as well as professional life. Like:
1. Winner of many hackathons.
2. Best student of the year.
3. Tag of "AI Guru" and many more.

Legacy

His work helped millions of the people. He also did great work for Islam, promoting teachings of Islam.
He made organization that will work forever to help the humanity.
